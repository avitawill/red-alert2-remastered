How to Play

Goal

Destroy every enemy structure and unit. Deploy your Base Crawler into a Command Yard, then expand: Power Plants keep your base running, Refineries and Harvesters turn crystal fields into credits, Barracks and War Factories build your army.

Economy & power

Amber crystal is worth $1 per unit, azure crystal $2. Crystal spires regrow nearby fields. Capture neutral Drill Rigs with an Engineer for steady income. If power use exceeds supply, production slows, the tactical map shuts off and powered defenses go offline.

Veterancy

Units gain experience by destroying enemies — Veteran (★) units hit harder and take less damage; Elite (★★) units also repair themselves.

Touch controls

  • Tap a unit to select it; tap the ground to move; tap an enemy to attack.
  • Drag with one finger to scroll; pinch to zoom.
  • Long-press then drag (or turn on Box) to select a group.
  • Double-tap a unit to select all of that type on screen.
  • Hold a group button (1–5) to assign; tap to recall.
  • Hold a build icon to pause / cancel it.

Mouse & keyboard

  • Left-click select / command, drag to box-select, right-click to deselect, right-drag or edge to scroll, wheel to zoom.
  • Ctrl+1-9 assign group, 1-9 recall (twice to center).
  • A attack-move · S stop · G guard · D deploy · X scatter
  • Q select army · E same type · H base · Space last alert
  • R repair mode · Z sell mode · Tab next build tab · Esc cancel / menu · P pause
  • Shift-click a unit icon to queue 5. Right-click an icon to hold, again to cancel.
